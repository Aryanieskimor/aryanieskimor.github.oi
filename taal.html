Batangas Province — The Complex Caldera

Key Information Card

Elevation 311 meters
Volcano Type Complex Caldera
Eruption Count 38 Historical
Distance to Manila ~50 km South

Unique Nestled Geography

Taal Volcano is famous for its unique layout: an active volcanic center on Volcano Island inside Taal Lake, which fills a prehistoric caldera complex.

Despite its modest height, Taal is classified as a Decade Volcano due to its proximity to heavily populated municipal regions.

Volcanic Hazards & Preparedness

Taal's eruptions are frequently phreatomagmatic (water-magma interaction), causing steam-driven ash plumes, volcanic smog (vog), and base surges. Major eruptive activity in January 2020 displaced over 300,000 residents across Southern Luzon.
