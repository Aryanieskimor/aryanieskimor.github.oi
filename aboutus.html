Our Mission

Situated along the Pacific Ring of Fire, the Philippine archipelago is home to over 24 active volcanoes and dozens of inactive peaks that shape the nation's landscapes, history, and ecosystems. Our mission is to provide students, travelers, and science enthusiasts with an accessible, educational resource covering these natural marvels—from the world-famous perfect cone of Mayon Volcano to the unique caldera system of Taal.

What We Offer

  • Volcano Catalog : Comprehensive profiles detailing active, potentially active, and inactive volcanoes across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao.
  • Eruption Historie : Chronological timelines and geological facts about historic volcanic events like the 1991 Pinatubo eruption.
  • Trekking & Safety Guides : Essential guidelines for eco-tourism, hiking permits, and zone safety precautions.

Data & Disclaimers

Information on this site is curated for educational and informational purposes using publicly accessible data from the Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology (PHIVOLCS).

Notice: For official volcanic hazard alerts, real-time status updates, and emergency bulletins, please refer directly to the official PHIVOLCS portal.
